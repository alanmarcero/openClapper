# Changelog

## v3.12.2

- **Parameter Locking:** Add per-parameter locking across every plugin parameter, persisted in session state and preserved across preset loads and randomization.

## v3.12.1

- **UI & Suite Fixes:** UI polish, named keyboard-ready padlocks, unit formatting and typed-value entry, per-slot A/B undo, inline preset confirms, 40x40 preset steppers, and screen-reader titles.

## v3.12.0

- **Micro-Crowd Doubler:** Randomizes 0.8–4.5 ms taps per MIDI hit with dispersive allpasses.
- **Flam Bursts:** Uncorrelated per-hand noise streams with equal-power panning.
- **Stereo Body:** Wide detuned stereo knock bloom and chest thump.
- **Palm-Cavity Resonators:** Coupled 2-pole modal resonators decoupling decay time from bandwidth.
- **True Velvet Noise:** Jittered grid regularity with a flat spectrum across sample rates.
- **Gabor Transients:** Minimum-phase Gabor chirps for Needle and Crack transients without IIR smear.
- **ImpactPunch:** Fast 2 ms log-domain transient compression with oversampled ADAA saturation.
- **GatedStereoReverb:** Eliminates comb pitch warble and tracks exact RT60 with HF damping.
- **SlapbackDelay:** 4-point Hermite interpolation, smooth tempo glide, and 150 Hz–6.5 kHz bandpass ADAA feedback.
- **DistortionShaper & BrickwallLimiter:** 4x oversampled DC-blocked ADAA2 waveshaping and 64-sample true-peak lookahead limiting that preserves pre-clap flams.
